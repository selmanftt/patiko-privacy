Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Patiko is designed for children ages 3-6. We do not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no chat and no user-generated content.

1. Information We Collect

Patiko does not collect, store or share personal information from its users, including children. We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, videos or location.

2. Data Stored on the Device

Game progress (stars, animal cards, the daily reward streak, the world built in "Build My World") and settings (language, sound, daily time limit) are stored only on the device. This data is never sent to us or to any server. Deleting the app deletes this data; parents can also reset progress in the Parent Panel.

3. Advertising

The free version shows advertisements through Google AdMob. All ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment (COPPA) and for users under the age of consent (GDPR), request non-personalized ads only and are limited to the "G" (general audiences) content rating. Patiko does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not track users across apps or websites. Google AdMob may process limited device information to deliver and measure ads; see Google's Privacy Policy. Premium users see no ads.

4. In-App Purchases

Patiko offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly and yearly) and an optional one-time lifetime purchase. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see or store payment information. Purchases and the Parent Panel are protected by a parental gate.

5. Speech and Sounds

Spoken instructions are generated on the device using the operating system's text-to-speech. No audio is recorded and the microphone is never used.

6. External Links

The app does not contain links for children. Only the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use can be opened from the parent-gated areas.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
